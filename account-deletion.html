RESILIOCURE

Delete your account

Transformed Arrogyam Private Limited · Developer of the ResilioCure app (package com.transformedarrogyam.resiliocure)

Option 1: delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open ResilioCure and sign in.
  2. Go to Me → Privacy → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and data are permanently deleted straight away.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you can no longer use the app, email kunal@transformed-healthcare.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my ResilioCure account”. We will confirm it is you and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, sign-in credentials and linked Google sign-in)
  • Profile and preferences
  • Daily check-ins, resilience scores, streaks, goals and achievements
  • AI Coach conversations
  • Reminder, privacy and support-request records

Information that stays only on your phone (for example coherence-timer notes and Health Connect readings) is removed when you delete the account in the app or uninstall it.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion. Deleted data may remain in encrypted database backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.
