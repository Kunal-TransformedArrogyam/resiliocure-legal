RESILIOCURE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026
Transformed Arrogyam Private Limited (“we”, “us”), developer of the ResilioCure app (com.transformedarrogyam.resiliocure).

1. What ResilioCure is

ResilioCure is a wellness and self-reflection app. You rate a few areas of your day (such as activity, nutrition, mood and rhythm), and the app turns your ratings into a resilience score, trends and suggestions. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. It is for adults aged 18 and over.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhy we use it
Account details: email address, sign-in credentials, or your Google account email and name if you sign in with GoogleTo create and secure your account and let you sign in on any phone
Profile: name, age, phone number, your health goal, preferred reminder timeTo personalise the app and reminders
Check-in answers: the 1–5 ratings you give each day and the scores, phases, streaks and trends calculated from themTo show your results and progress
Personalisation: answers to the setup quiz, the suggested actions you tick off or rate, goals and achievementsTo tailor your daily plan and recommendations

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and we do not use advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. Health Connect (Android)

If you choose to connect Health Connect, ResilioCure asks permission to read your step count and sleep sessions. We use this information only on your device, to suggest ratings for your daily check-in. We do not upload, store, sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising. Only the rating you confirm in your check-in is saved. You can disconnect at any time from the Integrations screen or in Health Connect settings, which removes our access.

4. The AI Coach

When you send a message to the AI Coach, the app sends the message, the recent conversation and a short summary of your latest check-in (your score and phase, your area ratings, patterns, today’s plan, streak, goal and the preferences you chose) to our server, which passes it to an AI service provider to write a reply. Your name, email address and phone number are not sent. The provider processes the data on our behalf to produce the reply. Coach replies are general wellness guidance and are not medical advice. If the AI service is unavailable, the app writes a reply on your phone from your own data instead.

Do not enter information in the coach that you do not want processed in this way.

5. Reminders and notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone. They do not require us to send you anything from a server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone’s notification settings.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers, who process it only to provide the app:

  • Supabase: secure hosting, sign-in and database for your account data.
  • An AI service provider: to generate AI Coach replies (see section 4).
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google” to sign in.

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people’s safety.

7. Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with access rules so that each account can only read its own data. Some information (such as your sign-in session and recent history) is also kept on your phone so the app works quickly and offline. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Privacy & security → Delete account. Your account and data are deleted straight away.
  • No longer have the app? Email us (below) from your account email and we will delete your account within 30 days. See how to delete your account.
  • Correct or update your details in Me → Edit profile.
  • Clear your coach conversation with the bin icon in the Coach tab.
  • Disconnect Health Connect in the app or in Health Connect settings.

After deletion, nothing that identifies you is kept. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten, and we may keep limited records where the law requires it.

9. Retention

We keep your information while your account exists so you can see your history. When you delete your account, we delete it as described above.

10. Children

ResilioCure is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
